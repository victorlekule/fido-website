Who We Are

We are FIDO.

Ficus Development Organization is a Tanzanian non‑profit working hand‑in‑hand with vulnerable communities to break the cycle of poverty. From climate‑smart farming to digital literacy, our integrated approach ensures no one is left behind.

  • Climate-Smart Farming
  • Youth & Women Empowerment
  • Digital Literacy & Innovation
