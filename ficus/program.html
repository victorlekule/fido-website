Lasting Change

Six core programs that address the root causes of poverty and build self‑reliance across Tanzania.

Support a Program
Program 01 — Quality Learning

Unlocking Potential Through Learning

We improve access to quality education, vocational training, and life skills for children and youth. Our mission is to ensure learning translates directly into sustainable livelihoods.

Infrastructure & Resources: We renovate classrooms and provide essential learning materials (books, desks, and solar lighting) to rural schools in the Lake Zone.

Vocational Training: For youth out of school, we provide skills in carpentry, tailoring, and electrical works, paired with basic business management training.

Teacher Support: We collaborate with local government to provide pedagogical workshops, focusing on inclusive education for children with disabilities.

Program 02 — Community Health

Healthy Communities, Brighter Futures

We promote health education, disease prevention, and access to health insurance for underserved families. Good health is the foundation of every thriving community.

Maternal & Child Health: Training community health workers to support expectant mothers with prenatal care and nutritional guidance.

Health Insurance Advocacy: Facilitating enrollment in the Community Health Fund (CHF) to ensure medical emergencies don't lead families into bankruptcy.

WASH Initiatives: Constructing clean water points and promoting hygiene in schools to reduce water-borne illnesses.

Program 03 — Sustainable Farming

Growing Resilience from the Ground Up

Through climate‑smart training and agribusiness support, we help smallholder farmers increase yields and secure their families’ food and income.

Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA): Training on conservation tillage, crop diversification, and irrigation systems to fight erratic weather patterns.

Market Linkages: Connecting smallholder cooperatives directly with wholesalers in Mwanza to ensure fair pricing for their produce.

Women in Agri: Specialized groups for female farmers to gain land-use rights and financial literacy.

Program 04 — Nature Conservation

Protecting Nature, Securing Tomorrow

We lead community‑based conservation and environmental awareness projects that restore ecosystems and build climate resilience.

Agroforestry: Integrating tree planting with farming to improve soil health and provide sustainable firewood sources.

Clean Energy: Promoting the use of fuel-efficient stoves to reduce deforestation in rural districts.

Conservation Clubs: Establishing environmental clubs in primary schools to raise a green-conscious generation.

Program 05 — Digital Inclusion

Innovation for Inclusion

We integrate AgriTech and EdTech to bridge the digital divide. From mobile apps for farmers to digital classrooms, we make tech a tool for equity.

Digital Centers: Establishing internet-enabled hubs in Mwanza where youth can learn coding and digital marketing.

Data for Farmers: Using SMS platforms to provide real-time weather and market price alerts to rural smallholders.

GreenTech Support: Incubating local innovations that focus on renewable energy and water recycling.

Program 06 — Self‑Reliance

From Survival to Self‑Reliance

We support entrepreneurship and savings groups—especially for women and youth—to break the cycle of poverty permanently.

VICOBA Support: Strengthening Village Community Bank groups through training on book-keeping and loan management.

Youth Incubation: Providing small seed grants and mentorship to youth starting businesses in Mwanza city.

Value-Addition: Training farmers on food processing (like drying or packaging) to increase profit margins.
