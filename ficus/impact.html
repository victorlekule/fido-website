Our Journey So Far

2018

Foundation & First Seed Funding

FIDO officially registered in Tanzania, launched pilot with 50 smallholder farmers in Mwanza, introducing drought‑resistant seeds and agronomy training.

2020

Education Initiative Launch

Construction of first classroom block in Ngara District began, funded by community contributions and international partners. 150 students enrolled in the first year.

2022

Health & Nutrition Program

Launched health insurance access program, enrolled 150 families, and established five community health outposts.

2025

Scaling & Replication

Expanded operations to three new regions. Today FIDO supports over 5,000 students, 1,200 farmers, and 800 young entrepreneurs across Tanzania.

“FIDO gave me a future, not just tools.”

— Fatima M., tomato farmer & market supplier, Mwanza

After joining FIDO’s training, Fatima’s harvest tripled. She now employs five women and supplies three local markets.
