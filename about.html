Our DNA

The Values That
Drive Our Mission.

These six pillars are the non-negotiable standards that guide every partnership, project, and decision we make at FIDO.

01

Transparency & Accountability

FIDO is committed to responsible stewardship of resources through strong financial management systems, independent auditing, participatory decision-making, and open communication with stakeholders, beneficiaries, and partners.

02

Integrity and Ethical Conduct

The organization promotes professionalism, ethical leadership, and responsible conduct. FIDO ensures that resources are utilized solely for development objectives and maintains confidentiality and trust in organizational operations.

03

Sustainability

FIDO promotes environmentally responsible and long-term development solutions. The organization integrates climate-smart agriculture, environmental conservation, and sustainable livelihood strategies to ensure lasting community impact.

04

Inclusivity & Equity

FIDO ensures that development interventions are inclusive, participatory, and equitable, prioritizing vulnerable and marginalized populations including women, children, youth, and disadvantaged communities.

05

Empowerment and Capacity Building

The organization focuses on strengthening knowledge, skills, and self-reliance among communities to enable sustainable socio-economic transformation.

06

Community-Centered Development

FIDO promotes community participation, local ownership, and culturally responsive development solutions designed to address real community needs.

07

Good Governance and Democracy

FIDO operates under strong governance principles characterized by participatory leadership, transparency in decision-making, defined institutional roles, and accountability mechanisms.

08

Collaboration and Partnership

FIDO recognizes partnerships as essential for sustainable development and actively collaborates with government institutions, NGOs, research institutions, private sector actors, and community organizations.
